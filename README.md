# ⚽ Football HQ

A responsive, all-in-one football hub featuring club exploration across major leagues, sample fixtures, league tables, favourite club tracking, mini-games (penalty shootout, quiz, score predictor, word scramble, tactics formation builder), and a football learning centre.

## 🌐 Live Website

- **URL:** [https://vijaykd.github.io/football-hq/](https://vijaykd.github.io/football-hq/)

---

## 🚀 Features

- **🏟️ Explore Clubs:** Search and filter teams across Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, and more.
- **📅 Matchday Centre:** Sample fixtures and matchups.
- **📊 League Tables:** Sample standings and statistics.
- **⭐ Favourites:** Save and manage favourite clubs locally in your browser.
- **🎮 Football Arcade:**
  - ⚽ Penalty Shootout
  - 🧠 Club Trivia Quiz
  - 🔮 Score Predictor
  - 🔤 Word Scramble
  - 🧩 Tactics Board & Formation Builder
- **📚 Football Learning Centre:** Explanations of rules, tactical concepts, glossaries, and Wikipedia links.
