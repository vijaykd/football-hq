Quick football glossary

Short definitions to help you understand commentary.

Offside

A player may be penalised if they are in an offside position when a teammate plays the ball and become involved in active play.

Pressing

Players move quickly to pressure opponents and try to win the ball back.

Counter-attack

A fast attack launched just after winning possession, often while the other team is out of shape.

Clean sheet

When a team finishes a match without conceding a goal.

Hat-trick

When one player scores three goals in a match.

Assist

A pass or touch that directly sets up a goal, according to the competition's statistics rules.
